Tooth library of Quick DICOM Planning (teeth-bodyparts3d.json)
================================================================

The tooth crowns in teeth-bodyparts3d.json are made from BodyParts3D:

  BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS),

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en
  Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ (BodyParts3D / Anatomography)

Changes made to the BodyParts3D teeth:
  - each of the 28 permanent teeth cut across its long axis at the average crown height from the
    incisal edge / cusp tips, the root removed and the cut closed with a flat cap (crowns only);
  - wisdom teeth 18, 28, 38, 48 (not in BodyParts3D) derived from the second molars (92 %, set
    behind them);
  - turned into the library's axes, simplified (about 0.35 mm edges), numbered (FDI) and the cusp
    tips / incisal edges marked for the waxup.
  Scripts: scripts/make-bodyparts3d-crowns.py and scripts/make-tooth-library.py
  (https://github.com/amsaleh1998-spec/Quick-DICOM-Planning).
