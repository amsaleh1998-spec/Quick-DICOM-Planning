MPR tools

Mouse
  • Left click/drag – move crosshair
  • Trackpad? Switch Input → Trackpad and see the Trackpad guide
  • Hold Shift – crosshair follows the mouse
  • Ctrl+Alt+drag (or Rotate tool) – rotate the other planes around this view
  • Crop tool – drag the blue box edges
  • Wheel – scroll slices
  • Ctrl+wheel or right drag – zoom · Alt/middle drag – pan
  • R – ruler: click start, click end; drag ends to edit, Del removes the hovered ruler
  • M or ⤢ – maximize / restore view
  • 1 / 2 – switch between MPR / Reorient and Implant planning
  • Right drag can be switched to brightness/contrast in the toolbar

      Mouse
      • Trackpad? Switch Input → Trackpad and see the Trackpad guide
      • Draw curve: hold the left button and drag along the arch (points are placed automatically, release to finish), or click point by point
      • Axial: wheel = level, drag curve points, dbl-click curve = add / point = remove
      • Panoramic: wheel = slice position buccal/lingual, Alt+wheel = move along the curve, click = jump
      • Cross-sections: wheel = move 1 mm along the curve
      • Tangential: wheel = rotate 360° (around the selected implant)
      • Implant: drag body = move · drag side outline = diameter (tapered: apical half = apex Ø) · drag apical end = length (click the live value to type it)
      • C / A handles (selected implant): C rotates around the apex, A rotates around the platform
      • Ctrl+Z / ⌘Z undo implant changes (unlimited) · Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y redo
      • Eye / MUA buttons in the implant list show or hide an implant or its abutment
      • Esc deselect / cancel · Del delete implant · double-click a name in the list to rename
      • Cross-sections follow the selected implant when it moves
      • Ctrl+wheel or right drag zoom · Shift/Alt/middle drag pan · M maximize · R ruler

      3D render

      Drag = rotate · wheel = zoom · Alt/Shift/middle drag = pan · middle-click a surface = rotation centre there · double-click or ⟲ = reset view. The crop box also clips the 3D view.

      3Dconnexion SpaceMouse

      The model follows the cap: tilt/twist = rotate, push sideways = pan, pull up / push down = zoom. Left button = fit, right button = next view.

      Segmentation

      1. Set the 3D thresholds so the structures are separated.
      2. Add a segment, then click the structure in the 3D view (or in any slice).
      3. Still touching? Draw a cut through the contact.

        Separation cuts

          Nerve canal

            Click the centre of the canal on the cross-sections (scroll along the curve) and/or in the panoramic, in any order – the points are joined into a smooth tube shown in every view and in 3D. Implants report their distance to it.

            Intraoral scans

              Upper/lower jaw scans, antagonists and wax-ups (STL, PLY with colours, OBJ). Place each one in its original position, copy the alignment of a scan that is already aligned, or align it to the CBCT by points or surfaces.
