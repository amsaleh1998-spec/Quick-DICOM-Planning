        Manual – by threshold

        1. Set the lower threshold so the structures are separated.
        2. Add a segment, then click the structure in the 3D view or in any slice.
        3. Still touching? Draw a cut through the contact.

          Nerve canals

            Click the centre of the canal on the cross-sections or in the panoramic, in any order (Ctrl+Z takes a point back). Implants closer than 2 mm are flagged. An automatic canal segment can also be turned into nerve paths (Segments).
